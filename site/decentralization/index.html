Scope

What the artifacts establish.

Consensus artifact

Preserves raw CometBFT and Cosmos responses, ranked CSV, exact concentration metrics, one-third and strict two-thirds coefficients, manifest and checksums.

Inspect the preserved state ↗

MOLNFT artifact

Preserves contract calls, counters, deployed-code hashes, reconstructed BinaryCIF objects, coordinate checks, tabular results, manifest and checksums.

Inspect the preserved state ↗

On-chain records establish observable protocol state. They do not by themselves prove validator beneficial ownership, scientific uniqueness, source-annotation correctness or biological validity. Those questions require separate evidence. The article therefore cites the exact claims each artifact supports and no broader claim.
